# Why CloudRader? :material-lightbulb-on-outline:{ .main-color }

Managing team operations and organizational tools shouldn't require complex, proprietary enterprise software or fragmented spreadsheets.

---

## :material-check-all: Say Goodbye to Chaos

Organizations often face common infrastructure hurdles:

- **Monolithic vendor lock-in** with high subscription fees.
- **Data privacy concerns** when sensitive organizational data is stored on third-party clouds.
- **Disconnected workflows** where tools don't communicate or require separate login credentials.

**CloudRader solves this with open-source, interconnected tools designed for privacy, simplicity, and self-hosted control.**

---

## :material-shield-check: Benefits for Organizations

### :material-server-network: Complete Data Ownership
Self-hosting your services means your data never leaves your infrastructure. You control backups, retention policies, and access logs.

### :material-puzzle: Freedom to Choose
Because each CloudRader service is autonomous, you can deploy just one service or the entire suite without unnecessary overhead.

### :material-key-chain: Seamless User Experience
Integration with Keycloak provides single sign-on (SSO). Users log in once and access all permitted tools across your organization.

### :material-code-tags: Developer-Friendly
Every service exposes clean, documented REST APIs, making it simple to build internal integrations, scripts, and automations.

---

## :material-thought-bubble-outline: The CloudRader Ethos

CloudRader is inspired by the idea of riding into the world of cloud technologies and conquering its complexity step by step:

> *"We build open-source tools, experiment with distributed systems, and make internal infrastructure less painful."*
